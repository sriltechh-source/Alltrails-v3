Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Mana Hub. By using our platform, you agree to these terms.

1. Service Usage

Mana Hub provides local delivery, grocery, food, and service booking solutions in the Nirmal district. Users must be 18+ to place orders. All orders are subject to availability and delivery partner confirmation.

2. Orders & Payments

Orders are placed via WhatsApp and confirmed by our team. Payment is collected on delivery unless otherwise agreed. Prices may change based on market rates.

3. Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled before dispatch by contacting us on WhatsApp. Perishable items cannot be cancelled once confirmed.

4. Privacy

Your location and personal data are used solely for delivery purposes and are not shared with third parties without consent.

Privacy Policy

Mana Hub values your privacy. We collect only what is necessary to provide our services.

Data We Collect

Name, phone number, address, GPS location (with permission), and order history stored locally on your device via localStorage.

How We Use It

Solely for order fulfillment, delivery coordination, and improving your experience. We do not sell your data.

GPS / Location

Location is captured only when you browse our app and is used to pre-fill booking forms. It is never stored on our servers.

Mana Hub
Your Local Delivery & Services Platform

Mana Hub was founded with a simple mission: to connect the people of Nirmal district with reliable local delivery and services. From fresh groceries to tiffin delivery, event planning to parcel services — we do it all.

We work with local partners, delivery riders, and skilled workers to create employment and serve our community better every day.

❓ FAQs
What areas do you deliver to?
We deliver to Khanapur, Bhainsa, Mudhole, Nirmal, Dilawarpur, Mamda, Kubeer, Narsapur, Kaddam, Jukkal and surrounding areas in Nirmal district.
How do I place an order?
Simply browse our products, add to cart, and place your order via WhatsApp. Our team will confirm and deliver to your location.
What is the delivery time?
Same-day delivery in Khanapur and Bhainsa. Other areas within 24–48 hours. Tiffin delivery by 12 PM daily.
How do I subscribe to tiffin service?
Click "Book Tiffin via WhatsApp" or choose a subscription plan. Our team will set up your monthly subscription.
Can I cancel my order?
Yes, orders can be cancelled before dispatch by messaging us on WhatsApp. Perishable items cannot be cancelled once confirmed.
